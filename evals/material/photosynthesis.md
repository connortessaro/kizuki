# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. In plants it happens in the chloroplast.

The light-dependent reactions take place in the thylakoid membranes. They split water and release oxygen as a by-product.

# The Calvin cycle

The Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma. It uses ATP and NADPH from the light-dependent reactions to fix carbon dioxide into sugar.

# Cellular respiration

Cellular respiration releases the energy stored in glucose. Most of the ATP is made in the mitochondria.
