気付き

Teach it back.

Kizuki is a study tool that plays the student. You explain a concept in your own words, and Kizuki asks about what you got wrong, what you left out, and what you said vaguely. Each question quotes your course material.

気付き (kizuki) is Japanese for noticing, as in noticing a gap in what you know.

npx kizuki What you need first

How a session goes

  1. Add your material

    Slides, PDFs, Word and Excel files, notes. Kizuki reads the text and remembers the page, slide, or cell each passage came from.

  2. Confirm the concepts

    Kizuki turns each heading into a concept, and a small model suggests ideas inside each section. Kizuki only uses the ones you confirm.

  3. Explain one without your notes

    Write it the way you would explain it to a classmate.

  4. Answer the student’s questions

    Up to three rounds. Kizuki asks where your explanation disagrees with the material, what the material says that you left out, and what you meant by vague words.

  5. Confirm what you missed

    You tick the points you missed. After a clean session Kizuki waits twice as long before the next review; after a miss, the concept comes back tomorrow. If you set an exam date, Kizuki moves reviews before it.

It only quotes your material

If a study tool states a wrong fact, you learn the wrong fact. Kizuki's model can't write facts, quotes, or questions in its own words, and the code checks this before you see anything.

  • The model only points at sentences of your material. Kizuki shows those sentences word for word.
  • Kizuki writes each question from a fixed template around the exact sentence, with a link to where it came from.
  • If nothing in your material matches what you wrote, Kizuki says “not in your material” and asks nothing.
  • When you and your material disagree, you decide. If the slide had a typo, you correct it, and Kizuki uses your version from then on.

It stays on your computer

You don't need an account. Kizuki runs a small model through Ollama, and it keeps your files and history in one folder you can copy or delete.

  • Your data: ~/.kizuki
  • Default models: qwen3.5:2b and nomic-embed-text
  • You can use MLX on Apple chips, or a hosted model. Before Kizuki sends anything off your computer, Settings asks you to tick that you understand.

Set up

  1. Install Node.js and Ollama

    Node.js 22.18 or newer, and Ollama.

  2. Download the two models

    ollama pull qwen3.5:2b
ollama pull nomic-embed-text

  3. Start Kizuki

    npx kizuki

    It checks the models, starts, and opens the dashboard in your browser. To keep it installed, run npm install -g kizuki and start it with kizuki.
