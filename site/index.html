Kizuki personal alignment assistant

Catch the work mismatch before you send the wrong update, merge the wrong assumption, or miss the deadline risk.

Kizuki reads your meetings, threads, tickets, and PRs into a local markdown vault. It notices what changed while you were busy: the quiet scope cut, the stale UAT date, the fixture-backed feature someone is about to treat as production-ready.

$ ./kizuki check "We are ready for UAT."
critical: vault says UAT slipped and the timeline still needs an owner.
