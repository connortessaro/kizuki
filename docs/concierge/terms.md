# Kizuki founding cohort — terms

> **Draft — not reviewed by a lawyer.** This is a plain-language summary of how
> the founding cohort works, not a contract. If you want a formal agreement in
> place before you pay, say so and we'll do that.

## Term and billing

- Month-to-month. No annual commitment.
- $49–99 per month, founding price, locked for as long as you stay in the cohort
  continuously. If the price rises later, yours doesn't.
- Cancel anytime, effective at the end of the current month. No cancellation fee.

## What's included

- A dedicated Kizuki instance.
- Onboarding: connecting three to five sources and configuring your Pack.
- A weekly review call, about 30 minutes.
- Direct support from the founder between calls.

## Data handling

- Your instance runs where you choose: a dedicated instance we operate for you,
  or your own infrastructure.
- Source access is read-only and delegated. Kizuki never sends messages or
  changes your source systems.
- We don't want your data. We don't sell it, we don't train outside models on
  it, and we don't use it for anything beyond running your instance and the
  support we agreed to.
- On exit, we delete everything: your instance, its data, and any derived
  indexes. You can export your vault first, and deletion is confirmed in writing.

## What's not included yet

- No SLA or uptime guarantee. This is a founding beta.
- No compliance certifications (no SOC 2, HIPAA, or ISO). If you have a hard
  compliance requirement, tell me before we start.
- Support is direct from me, the founder, not a 24/7 team. Fast, but human hours.

## Cadence

- Weekly review call, about 30 minutes, scheduled with you.
- Async support between calls.
